Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameWatchTonight ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

Cookies

We and our partners use cookies for analytics and to serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to function.

We link to third-party streaming services. When you click those links and sign up, we may earn a commission. Those services have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to read.

Your rights

Depending on your state (including under the CCPA/CPRA), you may request that we delete any data associated with you. Contact us at privacy@gamewatchtonight.com.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
